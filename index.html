01 / Process

HOW IT WORKS

Four simple steps to bring a railway crossing safety concern to attention.

01

Identify the Crossing

Notice an unmanned crossing or a safety issue that needs attention.

02

Submit a Report

Enter the location, issue type, and a short description using the report form.

03

Share Location Details

Provide clear location information so the crossing can be identified.

04

02 / Why It Matters

THE SAFETY CHALLENGE

Clear information helps communities communicate concerns about railway crossing safety.

🚧
Unmanned Crossings

Some crossings may need additional safeguards, signage, or attention from responsible authorities.

📍
Location Awareness

Accurate location details help teams understand where a reported concern has occurred.

🤝
Community Reporting

A simple reporting process gives citizens a way to communicate observations for review.

03 / Submit a Report

REPORT A CROSSING

Reporting Guide

HELP US UNDERSTAND THE ISSUE

📍
Be specific about location

Include the village, nearest station, road, or a recognizable landmark.

📝
Describe what you observed

Share factual details such as missing signs, damaged barriers, or visibility concerns.

⚠️
Prioritize immediate safety

For an urgent danger, contact the relevant railway or emergency authorities directly. This demo is not an emergency service.
